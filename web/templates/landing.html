The part other bots don't do

Most people decide whether a server is worth staying in during their first couple of weeks. {{ brand }} works that window deliberately.

  1. Someone joins. They quietly get a role named after that day, so everybody who arrived together forms a group.
  2. Eight days later. That group, and only that group, gets one ping in your ratings channel. It deletes itself two seconds later, so they get the notification without the clutter.
  3. They rate the server. One tap, 1 to 10. You read the results with a command, or right here on the dashboard.
  4. You find out if it worked. Retention is tracked per joining group, so a bad week is visible instead of averaged away.

Better retention is also one of the things Discord weighs when deciding which servers to surface in Server Discovery, so keeping people around helps your chances of being listed.
