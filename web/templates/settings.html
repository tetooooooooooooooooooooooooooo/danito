New account protection

Turn away accounts made in the last few days. A raid is nearly always accounts registered minutes earlier, so this stops most of one before a single filter has to fire.

Only sent on a kick. Without it a rejection looks like it was about them rather than about how old the account is, and they've no way to know they can come back. A ban isn't an invitation to return, so nothing is sent.

Punishments

A short note that deletes itself, so nobody reposts the same thing three times wondering where it went.

Past the limit, kicks and bans become timeouts instead and the case says why. It stops one wrong word on the banned list emptying your server.
