  1. Post the ratings survey

    Go to the channel you want it in and run /setchannel. That's the main feature: everybody who joins gets a quiet reminder about a week later, comes back to rate the server, and you get a number that Discord Discovery actually cares about.

  2. Build your log channels

    Run /logging setup anywhere. It makes a category of log channels, hidden from everybody but staff, and switches the whole server log on in one go.

  3. Everything else, whenever you like

    Welcome messages, autorole, role buttons, automod and moderation are all optional and all off until you turn them on. /help lists the lot.
